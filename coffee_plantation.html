Coffee Production Process

    Our coffee goes through five careful stages

  1. Planting-selecting and planting high-quality seedlings
  2. Pruning and Maintenance-caring for the young trees
  3. Harvesting-Hand picking ripe coffee cherries
  4. Processing and Drying-Washing and sun drying the beans
  5. Packaging-Sorting,grading and packaging for market

Plantation Activities

Pruning
Regular cutting of excess branches to improve air circulation, sunlight penetration,and to increase coffee yield and quality
Harvesting
picking ripe coffee beans
Drying
Spreading coffee beans under the sun for several days until the moisture content is perfect for storage
